An introduction to what I do

Websites, ranking and automation
for local businesses

Three services, and they are meant to work as one system: a site worth landing on, search results that send people to it, and follow-up that runs without you.

Custom websites

Fast, hand-built sites designed around your business rather than dropped into a template. Built mobile-first, because that is where most people will find you, and built to turn a visitor into a phone call or a form submission rather than just look nice.

  • Design built around your existing logo and brand colors
  • Custom sections and page count, not limited to a template
  • A contact or quote form that reaches you the moment someone fills it in
  • Domain connected, SSL installed, so nobody sees a "Not Secure" warning
  • Page titles and descriptions written for search from day one

Ranking optimization

A site nobody can find is a brochure in a drawer. This is the work that puts you in front of the people already searching for what you sell — technical audits, on-page optimization, and a content plan that moves you up the results page and keeps you there.

  • A technical audit first: speed, mobile, indexing, anything blocking you
  • On-page work against the searches your customers actually type
  • Local search set up properly, so you show up in your own city
  • Content planned around what you want to be found for

Marketing automation

Automation saves the hours you are currently spending on chasing. Email flows, lead capture and follow-ups that run themselves, so the enquiry that arrives at nine at night still gets answered at nine at night.

  • Lead capture that puts every enquiry in one place instead of three inboxes
  • Automatic replies and follow-up sequences so nothing goes cold
  • Booking and reminder flows that cut down no-shows
  • Set up once, then left running — you spend your hours on the actual work
