How we work

What happens in your first 30 days, what you get every month, what we promise and what we never will. Keep this page. It is the same for every client.

Your first 30 days

At kickoff we agree a written launch list for your plan. Everything on it is live by day 30.

1

Day 1

Agreement signed, card on file, and you send us what we need (the separate checklist). We start the texting registration, which takes one to four weeks.

2

Week 1

Your Google profile set up in your name, with real photos and correct details everywhere. Your review sprint starts. We record your starting numbers with the date.

3

Week 2

Website built or fixed, if your plan includes it. Tested on a phone.

4

Week 3

Fast replies and follow ups switched on and timed from a phone.

5

Week 4

Review requests running after every job. Your first one page report.

Our guarantee

Everything on your 30 day launch list is live by day 30, or you do not pay for month two until it is. The clock pauses while we wait on you for photos, access or your customer list, or on a carrier for texting approval. We tell you by email the day it pauses and the day it starts again.

Every month

One page, on the same day every month, in plain words. It shows:

  • Calls, searches and direction requests from your Google profile
  • New reviews, your total and your average
  • Where you show up on the map for the searches that matter to you
  • How fast new leads heard back
  • What we did that month, and what comes next

Around day 80 we put your first three reports next to your starting numbers and ask one question: what should month four focus on?

What we promise

  • The work on your plan, done on time
  • A one page report every month
  • A reply within one business hour on weekdays. You talk with our team, not a call center.

What we never promise

A ranking, a number of reviews, leads, appointments or jobs, or any savings or revenue figure. Nobody controls those, and anyone who promises them is guessing. We control the work and we show you what changed.

What is yours

Your website, its files and domain, your Google profile, your reviews, photos, customer lists and every account stay in your name. If you leave, everything stays with you. Nothing to buy back.

Payment and terms

  • Setup is paid before kickoff. Your first monthly payment is 30 days after kickoff, then on autopay.
  • Foundation is month to month. Owned Demand and Growth Partner have a 90 day minimum, then month to month.
  • After that, either side can end it with 30 days written notice by email. No fee to leave.
  • Move up any time. The setup you already paid counts in full toward the next plan.
  • On Growth Partner you pay Google directly for ads. Nothing passes through us, so there is no markup.

Reviews and introductions

Every customer gets the same direct link to your public review page. We never steer unhappy customers away from it, and nothing is ever given in return for a review. When your report shows movement, we may ask you once for a Google review of Weyney Media and one introduction. Neither is required.
