Day one checklist

What we need from you

Send these in the first week and your launch list stays on track. The 30 day guarantee clock pauses while we wait on any of them. Send it all to hamud@weyney.com, or text photos to 612-547-8650.

To start

  • The signed agreement and a card or bank account on file for autopay.
  • Owner access to your Google Business Profile. Add hamud@weyney.com as a manager. If you cannot find the profile or someone else controls it, tell us and we will sort it out with you.
  • Domain and website access. The login for where your domain is registered, and for your website if you have one.
  • Your logo, in the best file you have.

So people see your real work

  • Ten real photos of your crew, your trucks and finished jobs. Phone photos are fine. No stock photos.
  • Anything we should never use: photos, claims or names you do not want online.

So we can answer fast

  • Who answers new leads, which phone and which inbox they should reach, and your real hours.
  • Your phone carrier, so we can set up the text back on missed calls.

So your past customers can help

  • Your last 30 customers (first name, phone, email, job date) for the free review sprint.
  • Your past customer list, as much as you have, for the quarterly campaign. We only ever contact people who already did business with you, and every message carries a way to opt out.

Solar installers, also

  • Your financing partner and which options you offer (cash, loan, lease, PPA, prepaid lease), in writing, so the financing page says only what you have signed off.
  • The battery products you install, so the backup page names real products.
